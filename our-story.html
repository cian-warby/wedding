Our Story

Two Roads, One Journey

Cian grew up on the banks of the Shannon in Athlone, Ireland. Rand grew up among the ancient stone streets of Jerusalem, Palestine. Two very different childhoods, on two different edges of the map.

Life brought us both to Dublin — and that's where our story really began. Now, we're bringing our two families and all our favourite people together in Cantabria, Spain, to celebrate the next chapter.
