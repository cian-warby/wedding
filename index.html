Our Story

Two Roads, One Journey

Cian grew up on the banks of the Shannon in Athlone, Ireland. Rand grew up among the ancient stone streets of Jerusalem, Palestine. Two very different childhoods, on two different edges of the map.

Life brought us both to Dublin — and that's where our story really began. Now, we're bringing our two families and all our favourite people together in Cantabria, Spain, to celebrate the next chapter.

FAQ

Good To Know

Venue & Logistics

What is the address of the venue?

Palacio de Caranceja — Bo. Caranceja, 33, 39591 Reocín, Cantabria.

Will the ceremony be indoors or outdoors?

We're hoping for beautiful weather, so the ceremony and cocktail hour will take place outdoors. Dinner and dancing will then continue indoors.

Is there parking available?

Yes, there will be parking available.

Will transportation or shuttle buses be provided?

Yes! We will be providing a shuttle service from the venue to Santander city centre. Please let us know where you'll be staying when you RSVP so we can ensure you're included in the transportation arrangements.

Is the venue wheelchair accessible?

Yes, the venue is wheelchair accessible.

Dress Code

What is the dress code?

Black tie.

What kind of shoes are best for the venue?

We recommend choosing footwear that is comfortable and suitable for walking on grass. For ladies, block heels or wedges are great options. Gentlemen can wear dress shoes as usual. Most importantly, wear shoes you'll be happy dancing in all night!

Travel & Accommodation

Which airport should I fly into?

Santander Airport is the closest airport to the venue. Alternatively, you can fly into Bilbao Airport and enjoy a scenic trip through northern Spain on your way to the wedding.

Where should I stay?

Our venue also has a hotel with a limited number of rooms available. If you'd like to stay on-site, please visit the venue's website to book. Alternatively, Santander is just 20 minutes away and offers a range of accommodation options. For more recommendations, visit our Things To Do page.

Do we have a hotel discount code?

Yes! If you're staying at our venue (Palacio de Caranceja), use the code OLLIE when booking to receive 10% off your stay.

Social Media Etiquette

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We kindly ask you to enjoy the special moment and put your phones away.
