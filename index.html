Our Story

Two Roads, One Journey

Cian grew up on the banks of the Shannon in Athlone, Ireland. Rand grew up among the ancient stone streets of Jerusalem, Palestine. Two very different childhoods, on two different edges of the map.

Life brought us both to Dublin — and that's where our story really began. Now, we're bringing our two families and all our favourite people together in Cantabria, Spain, to celebrate the next chapter.

FAQ

Good To Know

Where is the wedding taking place?

The ceremony and reception will both be held at Palacio de Caranceja, a 17th-century palace set in the countryside of Cantabria, in northern Spain.

What is the dress code?

Formal attire. Evenings can turn cool, so something you can layer over a dress or suit is a good idea.

How do I get to Cantabria?

The closest airport is Santander (Seve Ballesteros Airport), about 25 minutes from the venue. Bilbao Airport is a further option, roughly 1.5 hours away. A shuttle will run between central pick-up points and the venue on the day.

Can I bring a plus-one, and are children welcome?

Please check your invitation for the names included — if you have any questions at all, just reach out to us directly.

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence means the world to us — registry details, if any, will be shared closer to the date.

Who should I contact with questions?

Feel free to reach out any time at hello@randandcian.com.
